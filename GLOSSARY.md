# ske27-view

A password-protected live map that shows where Servants currently are during a move, and where they are headed.

## Language

**Servant**:
The unit tracked on the map, either a single person or a couple, shown under one name.
_Avoid_: Person, traveler, participant, user

**Move**:
The relocation that the Servants are taking part in. It is not a trip or a journey.
_Avoid_: Trip, journey, route

**Current location**:
Where a Servant is right now. Every Servant has one.
_Avoid_: Position, place

**Destination**:
Where a Servant is headed, if known. Each Servant has their own, and Destinations are never shared.
_Avoid_: Target, waypoint, goal
